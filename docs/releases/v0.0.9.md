## BrushLLM Image Studio 0.0.9

- The redesigned desktop workspace keeps the tools on the left and the image, editing controls and export together on the right. The new crop-frame icon matches the deep teal, warm white and copper palette.
- Local tools share import, preview and output settings. Crop previews use a smaller raster while exports use the original pixel dimensions. Rounded-corner previews update without a full-size canvas and export transparent PNG or white-corner JPEG.
- Free local editing and batch export run on your device. Optional online AI requires your API key, explains what is sent before submission and is billed by usage.
